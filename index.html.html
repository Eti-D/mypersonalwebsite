Building KILEN’s brand communications structure
KILEN Global — Pioneer Communications Manager
Challenge

KILEN Global, a closed-membership leadership network, had no communications infrastructure in place. Growing and retaining its membership base required a structured system to plan, produce, and track content, both internally and externally, with nothing existing to build from.

Solution

Brought on as the network's pioneer Communications Manager to build the function from zero — designing a content framework and tagging system, managing the full content lifecycle, building and leading a content and media team, and directing communications strategy across campaigns and events.

Results
  • Designed and implemented end-to-end communications (internal and external) for 30+ webinars and virtual events, 3 annual leadership conferences each hosting 500+ participants, and 3 leadership campaign projects
  • Facilitated membership buy-in and sustained internal engagement, driving 250% growth in a closed-membership network, alongside an 80% increase in social media engagement within 6 months
  • First comprehensive content management structure implemented, improving reporting efficiency and brand consistency across the organisation
Read full story
KILEN Global needed more than content; it needed infrastructure, with the added complexity of a closed-membership model: growth here meant facilitating buy-in rather than simply broadening reach, while consistently maintaining interest among existing members. The engagement began with an audit of how the network communicated internally and externally, followed by the design of a content framework and tagging system that made resources easy to organise, track, and reuse. A cross-functional content and media team was recruited and trained to execute against this framework, with clear delegation, editorial review, and quality control built into the workflow. Communications strategy was then extended across the network's full calendar, ensuring every touchpoint reinforced the same narrative and consistently converted interest into membership. The result was a repeatable system that didn't just produce content, but scaled both visibility and membership growth in a closed network where every gain had to be earned through trust.
Organised and directed Parallax (a theatre production) in 3 weeks
Shekinah Theatre Production — Production Director
Challenge

A full theatre production needed to be delivered in a 3-week window, requiring two parallel tracks of execution: first, the internal end, which included rehearsals, story interpretation, internal logistics, effects, and membership welfare management. Then, the external side of things, which included fundraising, publicity, venue logistics, etc.

Solution

Took on production direction, managing both execution tracks simultaneously through a delegation-heavy management system, a targeted pocket-fundraising system built around specific brands and individuals of interest, and an intensive multi-channel publicity campaign.

Results
  • About ₦2 million raised in cash, plus endorsement materials valued at over ₦8 million
  • 2,000+ in-person attendees and 2,000+ virtual viewers across Facebook and YouTube
  • Full production delivered on schedule despite the compressed timeline
Read full story
The constraint wasn't getting the creative work project completed, but doing so within a very slim timeline given the scope of the project. With three weeks to turn a concept into a live production, the priority was building a system that could move fast without losing quality. A management structure was designed around clear delegation and rapid follow-up, allowing rehearsals, story development, and technical elements to progress in parallel rather than in sequence. Externally, a targeted pocket-fundraising system was designed around specific brands and individuals of interest, moving fundraising from opportunistic to deliberate. Publicity ran alongside this on multiple fronts, incorporating guerrilla stunts, flash mobs, and a broader publicity campaign, layered with conventional digital and physical publicity measures — to build anticipation and drive turnout. The outcome was a fully funded, well-attended, high-quality production delivered inside an almost impossible timeline, proof that speed and structure aren't mutually exclusive when the system is designed right.
Designing an award-winning entry for #LoveMyDad campaign
Men's mental health campaign entry — Strategy & execution
Challenge

An entry was needed for the #LoveMyDad men's mental health campaign, centred on making fathers feel seen, but the brand behind it had fewer than 500 followers, competing against personal brands with 20,000+.

Solution

A strategy built on three pillars — Impact, Visibility, and Scalability — combining first-person storytelling, targeted outreach to build early momentum, and a format designed to be easily replicated by others.

Results
  • 5,000+ views within 2 days, on a platform that typically underperforms for video content
  • Declared winner of the #LoveMyDad campaign
  • Over 200 direct feedback responses on the entry's impact, including people who went on to replicate a similar format to appreciate their own fathers and loved ones
Read full story
The follower gap made this a strategy problem before it was a creative one. Rather than compete on reach, the approach concentrated on emotional precision and distribution efficiency. The entry centred on a daughter writing and reading a letter to her father, walking through defining moments from birth to the present day, anchored around a moment where he did something extraordinary. This was designed to align the entry directly with the campaign's core idea of appreciating fathers and making them feel seen. Visual breaks were built in to increase viewer retention, while an original, non-aesthetical background and mobile-shot footage were used deliberately to heighten emotional authenticity and make the format feel easily replicable by anyone watching. A brand profile audit and perception refresh preceded the release to strengthen credibility, and a small, targeted outreach effort activated early supporters using a teaser clip to build momentum despite the limited following. The result was a win against far larger competitors, and a format simple enough to spur people’s desire to recreate it for their own fathers and loved ones.
Executive PR Strategy
Maternal health brand — Executive PR Strategy
Challenge

An executive director at a maternal health brand needed increased visibility and reputation on LinkedIn, in a landscape where executive thought leadership content is common and easily overlooked.

Solution

Designed an original content format — the Textual Breakfast Show — an article structured to simulate a live breakfast meeting, paired with a targeted publicity push around the release.

Results
  • 45% increase in visibility within 48 hours, measured through follower count, impressions, and profile views
Read full story
Standing out on a crowded platform required a format, not just a message. The Textual Breakfast Show was conceived as a distinct content experience; its structure and theme were designed to generate curiosity before the content was even read. The piece was titled "Pregnancy Begins with Him Too: How His Sperm Quality Shapes Her Pregnancy Experience," a title built to challenge assumptions and demand a second look. Publicity around the release leaned into that novelty, positioning both the format and the theme as the hook. The rapid, measurable lift in visibility within 48 hours confirmed the approach: on a platform saturated with conventional posts, a well-designed format paired with a provocative theme can outperform generic executive content.
